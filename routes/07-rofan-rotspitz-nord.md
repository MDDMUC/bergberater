## Why this line

Cable-car high limestone (~2000 m). Cooler than Kochel. Easy to pick a sunny south wall by accident — Rotspitz **Nord** is the shade list.

*Hosentöter* is the moderate north line: 5b, 3 pitches, rough fist/shoulder cracks then steep wall.

## Approach

Rofanbahn Maurach → walk the ski piste down, west toward Rotspitz (~45–60 min). Last steep switchbacks; right to east/north faces. North entries around the corner from the east face, a few minutes on steep grass.

Confirm last Bahn the morning you go.

## Descent

Rappel *Schattendasein* (Hosentöter / Nürnberger) or walk the normal route from the summit for east-face lines.

## Gear

- 60 m double/twin
- 10 draws
- **Larger cams** for Hosentöter / Nürnberger
- Helmet
