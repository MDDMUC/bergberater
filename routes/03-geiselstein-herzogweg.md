## Why this line

The Ammergau summer north wall. Long, shady, moderate grade. Famous for August heat. Not sport.

*Ballisto* on the south face is a heat-reject.

## Approach

Park Halblech, bike or bus toward Kenzenhütte, leave bikes at Wankerfleck. Normalweg ~45 min; before it peels right under the north face, follow tracks left ~10 min. Entry left of the wall under the big gully.

Plan 6–7 h car-to-car minimum; often more.

## Descent

Down-climb the normal route (UIAA II), ~60–70 min to the bike cache.

## Gear

- 60 m rope, 10 draws
- Slings + a few friends helpful
- Approach shoes, helmet, water 3 L (long day even in shade)
