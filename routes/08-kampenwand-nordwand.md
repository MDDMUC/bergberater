## Why this line

One of the few true north lines on a classic Munich mountain. East, Bahn, short. The famous west–east traverse is **not** a shady plaisir route.

## Approach

**Short:** Bahn to Bergstation, then under the Kaisersäle left to the north-face entries.

**On foot:** Hintergschwendt → Steinlingalm → same (~2 h).

## Descent

One 30 m rap into the Kaisersäle (**no loose rock onto people**). Walk back to Steinlingalm / Bahn.

## Gear

- 60 m rope (30 m rap)
- Draws
- Helmet
