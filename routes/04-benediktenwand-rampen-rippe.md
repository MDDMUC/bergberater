## Why this line

Closest serious north wall to Munich. All-day shade. Dry after this heat wave. Lower than Alpspitze, so still warm.

Harder bolted lines on the same wall: *Lebe deinen Traum*, *Cavemen* (6–7) — only if grade allows.

## Approach

Jachenau side toward Tutzinger Hütte. Entry almost behind the hut (10 min from the hut). Bike approach common.

## Descent

Alpine walk-off / normal routes — not a sport rappel grid. Check the bergsteigen page the night before.

## Gear

- 60 m rope, 8–10 draws
- Slings, basic nuts/friends
- Helmet
