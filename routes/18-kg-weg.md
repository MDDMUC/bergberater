## Why this line

Same high shady face as BW3, a notch more alpine. Grade fits. Use BW3 if you want more bolts.

## Approach

Osterfelderkopf, Nordwandsteig, left of Alpspitz-Plaisir / near BW3.

## Descent

Ferrata or Ostgrat + Nordwandsteig. Last Bahn 17:30.

## Gear

- 60 m rope
- Draws + slings, small rack
- Helmet
