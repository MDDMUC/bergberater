## Why this line

Chiemgau = Sunday-east. Grade 5 sits in the new band. Stadler lists it next to harder Hörndl lines as the moderate long one.

## Approach

Seehaus car park → Hörndlalm / north-face sockel, 1.5–2 h / 750 Hm (bike possible partway).

## Descent

West along the ridge to the main summit, then Hörndlalm. Or Schmidkunzweg down-climb (III).

## Gear

- 50–60 m rope
- 10 draws
- Slings, helmet
