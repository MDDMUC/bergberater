## Why this line

Real north face, homogeneous, good rock, mostly bolted, east of Munich for Sunday weather. Writers split: “perfectly bolted” vs “not for pure plaisir.”

Nearby: *Silenzio* (~6 / 5+/A0); *Leuchsführe* (2–3, serious classic).

## Approach

Kaindlhütte → Riegensteig toward Zettenkaiser → above the pines, left up the gully to the north face (~50 min from the hut). Entry ~50 m right of the big slab. Kaiserlift from Kufstein shortens the walk.

## Descent

Summit, then north via **Widauersteig** (easy via ferrata) to Kaindlhütte (1.5–2 h). Or south to Hintersteiner See if you started there.

## Gear

- 50 m rope
- Draws + **nuts and slings** (Stadler recommendation)
- Helmet
- Via-ferrata comfort for the descent (or treat Widauer as easy alpine)

## This weekend

Sunday only if you start early and are **down by early afternoon**. Longer than Sparchen.

## Do not

- Treat it as a 2-hour sport crag
- Stay on the face into the Sunday storm window
