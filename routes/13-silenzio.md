# Silenzio — Scheffauer Nordwand

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| Suitability rank | **7** |
| Massif | Kaisergebirge |
| Drive from Munich | 1:30–1:45 |
| Aspect | **North** |
| Grade | **UIAA 6** (one pitch, or 5/A0) + one 5−. Mostly 3–4 |
| Pitches / height | ~15 pitches / 520 m |
| Protection | alpine-bolted (E2). Hard bits well bolted; easy terrain sparser |
| Best day this weekend | Saturday |
| Status | go — at the grade cap |

## Why this line

Fits “max 6, only a few pitches there.” Directer than the Ostler, crosses it three times, finishes on it. True north.

## Approach

Same as Ostler: Kaindlhütte → helipad (~45 min). Entry ~50 m left, steep crack-corner with slings in the bolts, left of a nettle patch.

## Descent

Widauersteig to Kaindlhütte (~1 h).

## Gear

- 50 m rope
- Draws + nuts and slings
- Helmet

## This weekend

Saturday. First pitch can feel a solid 6. A0 the crux if you want to stay under 6.

## Do not

- On-sight the 6 if it is your absolute limit on the first pitch of a 15-pitch north face
- Sunday (too long)
