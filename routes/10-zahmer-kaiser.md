## Why this line

East-side bolted mid-grade multi-pitch exists. There is no single “the” north plaisir like Seebenwände.

**Cleanest shade option in this cluster is already card #6 (Sparchen).** Use this card only if you want a different Kaiser wall.

## Approach

Depends on the wall. Kaisertal / Vorderkaiserfeldenhaus / Kaiserlift are the usual gates. Sparchen is 5 min from the car.

## Descent

Per topo. Do not guess.

## Gear

Assume 60 m, 12 draws, helmet. Add a small rack if the chosen line is alpine.
