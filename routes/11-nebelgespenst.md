# Nebelgespenst — Nodlwand (Alpspitze)

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| Suitability rank | **2** (new, grade-first) |
| Massif | Wetterstein |
| Drive from Munich | 1:20–1:35 + Alpspitzbahn |
| Aspect | **East** (high: ~2050 m). Morning sun, afternoon shade |
| Grade | **UIAA 4** all the way |
| Pitches / height | 5 pitches / ~150–160 m |
| Protection | alpine-bolted (stands 2 bolts; intermediates partly bolted) |
| Best day this weekend | Saturday |
| Status | go |

## Why this line

Bolted in 2022 for first alpine multi-pitch days. Perfect grade. High and cool. Not due-north, but elevation does most of the heat work.

## Approach

Osterfelderkopf → short walk under the Nodlwand (15 min / 70 Hm), right of the Ferrata / Alpspitze north face.

## Descent

Rappel (60 m) or walk toward the Ferrata / Nordwandsteig. Last Bahn 17:30 Jul–Sep.

## Gear

- 60 m rope (40 m climbs; 60 m to rappel)
- 12 draws
- Helmet
- MSL kit
