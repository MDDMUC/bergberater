## Why this line

True north, modern bolts, half-day, short approach, Zugspitz backdrop. Best single match for a hot Munich weekend.

Neighbor *Prior-Gedächtnisweg* is harder (~7). Skip if 6+ on-sight is not in the bag.

## Approach

From the Ehrwalder Alm valley station follow signs for Seeben waterfall / Seeben-Klettersteig (~2 km, 200 Hm, 30–40 min). At the wall go right of the via ferrata, past a cave; entry shortly after.

Parking: Ehrwald / Ehrwalder Almbahn.

## Descent

Usually **rappel the route**. Optional fixed-rope exit toward Seebensee (steep, not trivial). Do not drop stones — parties below and via-ferrata traffic.

## Gear

- 60 m **double / twin** (rappels)
- 12 quickdraws
- Helmet
- No trad rack needed
