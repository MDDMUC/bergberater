## Why this line

Same hut as Ostler / Nordwandliebe / Silenzio. Grade 4. East wall = morning sun. Useful if the north face is seeping and you still want a Kaiser day.

## Approach

Kaindlhütte, then toward the Zettenkaiser (west neighbor of the Scheffauer).

## Descent

Per topo / hut paths. Confirm the night before.

## Gear

- 50–60 m rope
- Draws, slings, small rack
- Helmet

## Topo and map

- Mentioned as a worthwhile 4th-grade east wall on the Ostler page: https://www.bergsteigen.com/touren/klettern/ostler-fuehre-scheffauer/
- [OSM Kaindlhütte](https://www.openstreetmap.org/#map=14/47.567/12.183)
- Paper: Wilder Kaiser / Panico

## This weekend

Not the first pick. Prefer Nordwandliebe or Ostler on the true north face.

## Do not

- Treat this as a fully researched plaisir line — take a real topo
- Start it in the midday heat
