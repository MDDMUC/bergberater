## Why this line

Shortest quality bolted limestone on the east side. No gondola. Done before Sunday storms. Shade until mid-afternoon.

*Möwe Jonathan* is famous and well bolted — **too hard as the moderate objective**. Use the wall; pick easier pitches unless 7+ is in the bag. Alt start *'s kloane Feierabendteuer* (5+) skips the 7+ first move.

## Approach

From Kufstein toward Ebbs. ~400 m after the town sign, right toward the quarry, then **left** (do not drive into the works) to the Kaisertalstraße barrier. 2–5 min walk south to Sector A.

## Descent

Rappel the route. Either station-to-station with 50 m single (clip directionals on the diagonal raps) **or** one 60 m free-hanging rap from the tree on the botanic ramp. **Shorter than 60 m and you come up short in space.** Do not drop stones onto Sector B.

## Gear

- 60 m double/twin **or** 50 m single if you rap station-to-station
- 14 draws
- Helmet
