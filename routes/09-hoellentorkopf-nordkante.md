## Why this line

Same logistics as Alpspitze, cooler, moderate alpine ridge if *Alpspitz-Plaisir* is too hard or crowded. Route-finding errors are common — take the Deichjodler topo, not only Panico.

## Approach

Alpspitzbahn → down toward Hupfleitenjoch / Knappenhäuser, then left through pines and scree to the north edge. Not the “walk 20 min from the station” of the Plaisir lines. Budget real approach time.

## Descent

Toward Höllentor and back to Osterfelder. **Last Bahn 17:30 Jul–Sep** — missing it is a long walk to Hammersbach.

## Gear

- 60 m rope, draws, slings, basic friends
- Helmet
- Approach shoes, headlamp if you are slow
