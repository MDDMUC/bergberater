## Why this line

True north, moderate, long, classic (Ostler solo 1903). Stands rebuilt. Shade all day. Grade sits exactly in the new band.

## Approach

Kaiserlift or e-bike to Kaindlhütte, then ~45 min toward Widauersteig / helipad. Entry at a smooth groove-like corner. Lift from 08:30 — budget time.

## Descent

Widauersteig (easy via ferrata) to Kaindlhütte. Helmet on — rockfall.

## Gear

- 50 m rope
- 8 draws, long slings
- Basic nuts/friends
- Helmet

Saturday long north face. Snow can linger in the gully — after this heat wave it should be gone. After rain wait a dry day. Several parties = rockfall.
