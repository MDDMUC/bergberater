## Why this line

Highest easy-access north/NE limestone from Munich. Cable car kills the approach. ~10 °C cooler than the city.

**If 6+ is OK:** *Alpspitz-Plaisir*.  
**If you want moderate bolts:** *BW3* and **stop**.  
**Do not** treat *Adamplatte* as sport.

## Approach

Alpspitzbahn to Osterfelderkopf. 20–30 min on the Nordwandsteig. *Alpspitz-Plaisir* starts after the “Dachl” at the first tunnel (bolts visible right). BW3 is the better-bolted sockel under the Herzl.

Jul–Sep Bahn (lookup 2026-08-14): **08:00–17:30**, last uphill 30 min before close. Confirm: https://zugspitze.de/de/Service-Informationen/Betriebszeiten-Fahrplaene

## Descent

Ostgrat + Nordwandsteig (wired, steep) back to the Bahn. **Do not rappel Alpspitz-Plaisir** — Nordwandsteig and other parties below. Watch last gondola.

## Gear

- 50–60 m rope, 12–14 draws (some alpine)
- Helmet
- Approach shoes + poles if descending the Ostgrat
- Small rack only if you continue into Adamplatte
