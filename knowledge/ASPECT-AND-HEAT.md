# Aspect and heat

When the user says it will be hot, aspect is a **hard filter**, not a preference.

## Keep (shade list)

| Aspect | Shade window (summer, approx) |
|--------|-------------------------------|
| **N** | All day |
| **NW** | Morning into early afternoon (Sparchen ~until 14:00) |
| **NE** | Afternoon shade; morning sun possible |

High elevation (Osterfelder ~2050 m, Rofan ~1800–2200 m) is a second cooling lever. Combine with north when you can.

## Reject on heat weekends

S, SW, SE — even if the route is famous and perfectly bolted.

Known heat-rejects seeded in `destinations/INDEX.md`: Leonhardstein, Ruchenköpfe south, Geierwand Haiming, Steinplatte, Guffert south, Kampenwand south, Kopfkraxen south.

## Other heat rules

- North walls **seep** after rain. After a dry heat wave they are usually the driest they get.
- Descent ridges and car parks are still full sun — pack water.
- Dark limestone (Kaiser, some Wetterstein) stays cooler than pale south slabs but still cooks if it faces south.
