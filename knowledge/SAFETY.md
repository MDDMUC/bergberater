# Safety

## Abort rules (default)

- **Thunderstorms:** off the wall at the first building cu-nims, or by the hour written in the trip brief (Sunday 16 Aug 2026: aim to be down **by 13:00**).
- **Last lift:** if the pick uses a Bahn, write the last descent in the brief. Missing it is a long walk, not a shrug.
- **Heat:** 2–3 L water/person; start early; sun on descents.
- **Rockfall:** big north faces in a heat wave can shed. Helmet on. Don't linger under parties.
- **North seepage:** after rain, pick another wall. After a dry heat wave, north is usually OK.

## Protection honesty

Do not send a bolts-only party up an alpine classic. If the area file says alpine, say what extra they need (route-finding, maybe nuts/cams, walk-off).

## Rope

Assume 60 m until the user confirms. Many local rappels want **60 m double / twin** or a 60 m single with station-to-station raps. Call this out per route.

## Out of scope unless asked

- Via ferrata as the main objective
- Glaciers / hochtouren (DAV Aug 2026: still bad — rockfall, closed huts on some classic lines)

## Not medical / not a guide

This assistant plans. The party makes the go/no-go on the day.
