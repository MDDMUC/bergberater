## Ranked list (these dates, not forever)

1. **Seebenwände *Tirol Plaisir*** — N, plaisir, 6 pitches, UIAA 6+ crux. Best overall match.
2. **Alpspitze *Alpspitz-Plaisir* or *BW3*** — N/NE, high, Bahn. Plaisir crux 6+; BW3 is the moderate bolt-friendlier line. Adamplatte = alpine.
3. **Geiselstein Nordwand (Herzogweg)** — N, UIAA 5/5+, ~17 pitches, **alpine**. Saturday only.
4. **Benediktenwand Nord** — closest N wall. Classics alpine; sport lines 6–7.
5. **Scheffauer *Nordwandliebe*** — N, ~5−, alpine-bolted. Sunday east.
6. **Sparchen / Schanzer Wände** — NW until ~14:00, plaisir, 1:15 drive. Best Sunday logistics. *Möwe Jonathan* is 7+ — pick easier lines.
7. **Rofan** — high, cooler; **only a named north sector**. Saturday.
8. **Kampenwand Nordgipfel only** — Sunday east, short. Not the south traverse.
9. **Höllentorkopf Nordkante** — UIAA 4+, alpine, same Bahn as Alpspitze.
10. **Zahmer Kaiser MSL** — Sunday east; name the wall and aspect the night before.

Heat-rejects: Leonhardstein, Ruchenköpfe, Geierwand Haiming, Steinplatte, Guffert south, Kampenwand south.
