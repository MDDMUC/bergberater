## Why it is in the catalog

Shortest quality bolted limestone on the east side. No gondola. Done before Sunday storms.

## Named routes (seed — re-check live)

| Route | Grade | Pitches | Protection | Notes |
|-------|-------|---------|------------|-------|
| *Möwe Jonathan* | UIAA 7+ (one move; alt start 5+) | 6 / 140 m | plaisir | Too hard as the main moderate objective. Famous, well bolted. |
| Sport garden + easier MSL | 5–6 range | 1–several | plaisir | Use these unless 7+ is in the bag |

Rockfall onto the crag below — careful rappels (60 m free-hanging option exists; don't come up short).

## Approach / descent

Park Kaisertalstraße barrier (not into the quarry). 2–5 min to the wall. Rappel the route.

- Drop stones on Sector B
- Stay past mid-afternoon in a heat wave (sun arrives ~14:00)
