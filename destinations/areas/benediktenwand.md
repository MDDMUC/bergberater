## Why it is in the catalog

Closest big north wall to Munich. All-day shade. Dry after this heat wave.

## Named routes (seed — re-check live)

| Route | Grade | Pitches | Protection | Notes |
|-------|-------|---------|------------|-------|
| *Rampe–Rippe* | mostly easier, crux ~6/7 or A0 | long | alpine | Classic. Grass bands can stay wet after rain. |
| *Lebe deinen Traum* | 6–7 | long | sport-bolted alpine | Harder than moderate default |
| *Cavemen* | 6+ range | | sport-bolted in hard bits | Same |

## Approach / descent

Jachenau / Tutzinger Hütte side. Check current parking. Descent is alpine, not a sport rappel grid.
