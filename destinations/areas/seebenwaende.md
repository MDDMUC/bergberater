## Why it is in the catalog

Best single match for hot Munich weekends: true north, modern bolts, half-day, short approach, Zugspitz backdrop.

## Approach / descent

~30–40 min / 200 Hm from Ehrwald via waterfall / Seebensteig path. Usual descent: rappel the route. Optional fixed-rope exit toward Seebensee (steep, not trivial).
