## Why it is in the catalog

Classic Munich playground, east, short Bahn approach. The famous traverse is **not** a shady plaisir route.

| Route | Grade | Pitches | Protection | Notes |

## Approach / descent

Bahn to Bergstation. Minutes to the rock. Sunday: named north line only, down early.
