## Why it is in the catalog

Highest easy-access north/NE limestone from Munich. Cable car kills the approach. Air is ~10 °C cooler than the city.

## Approach / descent

Alpspitzbahn to Osterfelderkopf. 20–30 min to entries. Descent: Ostgrat + Nordwandsteig back to the Bahn, **or** last gondola. Jul–Sep schedule (lookup 2026-08-14): **08:00–17:30**, last uphill 30 min before close. Confirm the morning you go: https://zugspitze.de/de/Service-Informationen/Betriebszeiten-Fahrplaene
