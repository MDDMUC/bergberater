# Geiselstein Nordwand

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| Slug | geiselstein |
| Massif | Ammergauer Alpen |
| Drive from Munich | 1:20–1:40 toward Halblech / Wankerfleck |
| Aspect | **North** (also S/SW walls — ignore those in heat) |
| Protection typical | alpine (stands + key bolts) |
| Elevation | wall ~1400–1885 m |
| Best in heat? | **yes** on the Nordwand |
| Status | go — Saturday long day |

## Why it is in the catalog

The Ammergau summer north wall. Long, shady, moderate grade. Not sport.

## Named routes (seed — re-check live)

| Route | Grade | Pitches | Protection | Notes |
|-------|-------|---------|------------|-------|
| *Alte Nordwand / Herzogweg* | UIAA 5 / 5+ | ~17 / 400–500 m | alpine, bolted stands | Default long north line |
| *Ballisto* | 6+ | 5 | mixed | **South** — heat reject |

## Approach / descent

Wankerfleck is the usual north/west start (~30–45 min). Long day — Saturday only this weekend.
